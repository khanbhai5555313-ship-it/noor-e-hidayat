Ayatul Kursi: Arabic, English Translation and Benefits

Surah Al-Baqarah, Ayah 255

What is Ayatul Kursi?

Ayatul Kursi, also known as Ayat al-Kursi, is verse 255 of Surah Al-Baqarah. It describes the greatness, knowledge, power and perfect attributes of Allah.

Quran Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:255

Ayatul Kursi in Arabic

اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ مَنْ ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِمَا شَاءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَئُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ

Surah Al-Baqarah 2:255

Ayatul Kursi English Translation

Allah—there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all existence. Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what will be after them, and they encompass not a thing of His knowledge except for what He wills. His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, and their preservation tires Him not. And He is the Most High, the Most Great.

Reference: Quran 2:255

Virtues of Ayatul Kursi

Ayatul Kursi has great virtue in Islamic tradition. An authentic report describes Ayatul Kursi as the greatest verse in the Book of Allah.

It is also reported from Abu Umamah that the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said that whoever recites Ayatul Kursi after every obligatory prayer has a great promise of entering Paradise, with death being the only barrier.

Hadith reference: Sunan an-Nasa'i; authenticated by Ibn Hibban.

Why Read Ayatul Kursi?

Ayatul Kursi reminds Muslims of Allah's absolute power, perfect knowledge and complete authority over the heavens and the earth. Reading and reflecting on its meaning can help a believer remember Allah and strengthen faith.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ayatul Kursi found in the Quran?

Ayatul Kursi is found in Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 255.

What does Ayatul Kursi mean?

Ayatul Kursi describes Allah's oneness, eternal life, perfect knowledge, authority and greatness.

When can Ayatul Kursi be recited?

It may be recited as part of one's remembrance of Allah. A well-known hadith specifically mentions reciting it after every obligatory prayer.

Reference

Quran: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:255

Hadith: Reported from Abu Umamah regarding reciting Ayatul Kursi after obligatory prayers.
