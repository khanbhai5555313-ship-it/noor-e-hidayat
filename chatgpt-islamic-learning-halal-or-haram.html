How to Use ChatGPT for Islamic Learning: Is It Halal or Haram?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become a part of everyday life. Among the most popular AI tools today is ChatGPT, which can answer questions, explain difficult subjects, summarize information, translate languages and assist people with learning.

This has also raised an important question among Muslims: Can ChatGPT be used for Islamic learning? Is using AI to learn about Islam permissible, or is it impermissible?

The answer requires an important distinction. Using a tool and accepting everything that the tool says are two different things.

Use AI as an assistant, not as an authority.

What Is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is an artificial-intelligence system that can generate responses to questions and instructions. It can explain topics in simple language, organize information, translate text, create summaries and assist with research.

However, ChatGPT is not a human scholar and does not possess religious authority.

Most importantly, an AI system can sometimes provide an answer that sounds convincing while actually being incorrect. Important information should therefore be checked against reliable sources.

Is Using ChatGPT for Islamic Learning Permissible?

In general, using a technological tool for a permissible purpose is not automatically impermissible simply because it is technology or artificial intelligence.

The important question is: How is the tool being used?

If a person uses ChatGPT to understand difficult terminology, translate material, organize study notes, learn basic background information, prepare questions for a scholar or assist with research, it can be a useful educational tool.

However, there is a very important condition: Islamic information must be verified.

“So ask the people of knowledge if you do not know.”
— Qur'an 16:43

This teaches us the importance of referring to people of knowledge when we do not know something. ChatGPT can assist with learning, but it should not replace qualified Islamic scholarship.

Why Should We Be Careful With AI and Islamic Knowledge?

Islamic knowledge is not simply a collection of information. Understanding a Qur'anic verse, Hadith or legal ruling can require knowledge of Arabic, Tafsir, Hadith sciences, Fiqh and the opinions of qualified scholars.

AI may sometimes misunderstand a question or give an oversimplified answer to a complicated matter.

For example, ChatGPT might:

  • Give an incorrect Hadith reference.
  • Present a weak narration without sufficient explanation.
  • Confuse different narrations.
  • Combine opinions from different scholars.
  • Misunderstand a complicated Fiqh question.
  • Provide information that sounds reliable but needs verification.

Therefore, important religious claims should always be checked against reliable Islamic sources.

“And do not pursue that of which you have no knowledge.”
— Qur'an 17:36

What Can Muslims Use ChatGPT For?

1. Understanding Difficult Concepts

If an Islamic concept is difficult to understand, ChatGPT can help explain it in simple language.

For example, a student may ask: “Explain the meaning of Tawheed in simple English.”

The explanation can then be checked through reliable Islamic books and scholars.

2. Translating Islamic Texts

ChatGPT can assist with translation between languages. This can be particularly helpful for people who are learning English or Arabic.

However, translations of Qur'anic verses and Hadith should be checked against recognized translations and reliable sources.

3. Preparing Study Notes

Students can use AI to organize information into headings, bullet points, questions, answers, summaries and revision notes.

4. Preparing Questions for Further Study

ChatGPT can help a student identify questions that require deeper research or discussion with a qualified teacher.

5. Learning Arabic and Islamic Terminology

Students can use AI to understand the general meaning of Arabic words, Islamic terminology and basic grammatical concepts.

Advanced Arabic, Tafsir and other specialized Islamic sciences should still be studied with qualified teachers and reliable books.

Can ChatGPT Give Fatwas?

ChatGPT should not be treated as a Mufti.

A Fatwa can depend on personal circumstances, detailed facts, scholarly principles and differences of opinion. An AI system may not have all the information required to give an appropriate ruling.

Questions concerning marriage, divorce, inheritance, financial transactions and complicated matters of Fiqh should be taken to a qualified and trustworthy scholar or Mufti.

ChatGPT may help someone understand terminology or prepare questions, but it should not replace qualified human scholarship.

Can ChatGPT Be Used to Study the Qur'an?

ChatGPT can be used as an additional educational tool for studying general Qur'anic topics, provided that it is not treated as an independent authority.

A person may ask for a simple explanation of a verse or a summary of a topic, and then compare the information with recognized Tafsir works and reliable Islamic sources.

The Qur'an teaches us to ask people of knowledge when we do not know.

Can ChatGPT Be Used to Study Hadith?

It can assist with preliminary research, but Hadith requires particular caution.

Whenever ChatGPT provides a Hadith, check:

  • The exact wording of the narration.
  • The original source or Hadith collection.
  • The Hadith number where applicable.
  • The authenticity or grading of the narration.
  • The explanation given by qualified scholars.
Do not share a Hadith simply because an AI generated it. A beautiful quotation is not automatically a Hadith.

Benefits of Using AI for Islamic Education

  • Accessibility: People can ask questions and receive explanations at any time.
  • Language Assistance: AI can help with English, Arabic and other languages.
  • Organization: Study material can be arranged into useful notes.
  • Educational Support: Students can prepare questions and revision material.
  • Research Assistance: AI can help identify topics for further research.

What Are the Dangers?

One of the greatest dangers is false confidence. An AI-generated answer may sound extremely convincing even when it is wrong.

In religious matters, this can become serious if a person unknowingly spreads an incorrect Hadith, inaccurate Tafsir, fabricated quotation or wrong religious ruling.

Never confuse a fluent answer with an authentic answer.

A Safe Method for Using ChatGPT for Islamic Learning

Step 1 — Ask

Use ChatGPT to understand a topic, organize information or identify questions for further study.

Step 2 — Verify

Check Qur'anic verses, Hadith, references and scholarly opinions through reliable Islamic sources.

Step 3 — Ask a Scholar

If the matter involves a Fatwa, personal circumstances or a complicated question of Aqeedah or Fiqh, consult a qualified Islamic scholar.

What Should We Never Do?

  • Do not blindly copy an AI-generated Fatwa.
  • Do not assume every Hadith provided by AI is authentic.
  • Do not publish Qur'anic translations without checking them.
  • Do not attribute statements to scholars without verifying the source.
  • Do not deliberately create or spread false religious information using AI.
  • Do not allow AI to replace teachers, scholars and authentic Islamic books.

Is AI Itself Haram?

The existence of AI does not, by itself, make every use of AI Haram.

Like many other tools, its use depends upon its purpose and manner of use. Using technology for beneficial education is different from using it to spread falsehood or commit something prohibited.

Therefore, instead of simply asking:

“Is ChatGPT Haram?”

We should also ask:

“For what purpose am I using ChatGPT, and am I using it responsibly?”

The Golden Rule for Islamic AI Use

Use AI as an assistant, not as an authority.

Let it help you:

  • Understand
  • Organize
  • Translate
  • Research
  • Prepare questions

But when it comes to:

  • Fatwa
  • Hadith authentication
  • Complex Fiqh matters
  • Important religious rulings

Return to reliable Islamic sources and qualified scholars.

Conclusion

ChatGPT and other AI tools can potentially be useful for Islamic education when they are used responsibly.

They can help Muslims understand difficult subjects, organize studies, translate material, prepare questions and identify areas for further research.

However, AI should never be treated as a replacement for the Qur'an, authentic Sunnah, recognized Islamic scholarship or qualified scholars.

The safest approach is simple:

Use ChatGPT for assistance. Verify Islamic information. And when you do not know, ask the people of knowledge.

May Allah grant us beneficial knowledge, protect us from spreading incorrect information, and guide us to use modern technology in ways that are beneficial and pleasing to Him. Ameen.

Allah knows best.
