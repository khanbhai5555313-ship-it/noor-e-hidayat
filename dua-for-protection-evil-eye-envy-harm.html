Dua for Protection from Evil Eye, Envy and Harm

Authentic Islamic Duas from the Qur'an and Sunnah

Every believer wants to feel safe, peaceful and protected. Sometimes we worry about the evil eye, jealousy, envy, unseen harm or difficulties that we cannot control. Islam teaches us that protection ultimately comes from Allah alone.

The Qur'an and Sunnah teach believers to seek Allah's protection through sincere dua, remembrance, Qur'an recitation and trust in Him. These practices are not merely words; they are a way of turning the heart back to the One who controls every benefit and every harm.

In this article, you will find authentic Islamic supplications and Qur'anic passages for protection from the evil eye, envy and harm, together with Arabic, transliteration, simple English meaning and Urdu translation.

What Is the Evil Eye in Islam?

The evil eye, known in Arabic as Al-'Ayn, is affirmed in the Sunnah. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said that the influence of the evil eye is real.

This does not mean that a believer should become frightened of every person or suspect everyone around them. Rather, a Muslim should remain balanced: take the means of protection taught by Islam and place complete trust in Allah.

The Prophet ﷺ also permitted and instructed the use of ruqyah for the evil eye. This shows that seeking protection and healing through permissible Islamic supplications is part of the Sunnah.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2187, Sahih Muslim 2195a.

What Is Envy (Hasad)?

Envy, or hasad, is when a person dislikes another person's blessing and wishes that the blessing would disappear. Islam teaches believers to protect themselves from the harm of envy while also teaching them to purify their own hearts from jealousy.

One of the clearest Qur'anic protections against envy is found in Surah Al-Falaq, where Allah teaches us to seek refuge from the evil of an envier when he envies.

1. Surah Al-Falaq — A Powerful Qur'anic Protection

Surah Al-Falaq teaches the believer to seek refuge in the Lord of the daybreak from different forms of evil, including the evil of an envier.

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ
مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ
وَمِن شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ
وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ

Transliteration

Qul a'udhu bi rabbil-falaq.
Min sharri ma khalaq.
Wa min sharri ghasiqin idha waqab.
Wa min sharrin-naffathati fil-'uqad.
Wa min sharri hasidin idha hasad.

English Meaning

Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of the daybreak, from the evil of what He has created, from the evil of the darkness when it settles, from the evil of those who blow on knots, and from the evil of an envier when he envies.

Urdu Translation

کہہ دیجیے: میں صبح کے رب کی پناہ لیتا ہوں، ہر اس چیز کے شر سے جو اس نے پیدا کی، اور رات کی تاریکی کے شر سے جب وہ چھا جائے، اور گرہوں میں پھونکنے والیوں کے شر سے، اور حسد کرنے والے کے شر سے جب وہ حسد کرے۔

Reference: Qur'an 113:1–5 (Surah Al-Falaq).

2. Surah An-Nas — Seeking Protection from Hidden Evil

Surah An-Nas teaches us to seek refuge in Allah from the whispering evil that can affect the hearts of people and jinn.

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ
مَلِكِ النَّاسِ
إِلَٰهِ النَّاسِ
مِن شَرِّ الْوَسْوَاسِ الْخَنَّاسِ
الَّذِي يُوَسْوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ النَّاسِ
مِنَ الْجِنَّةِ وَالنَّاسِ

Transliteration

Qul a'udhu bi rabbin-nas.
Malikin-nas.
Ilahin-nas.
Min sharril-waswasil-khannas.
Alladhi yuwaswisu fi sudurin-nas.
Minal-jinnati wan-nas.

English Meaning

Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind, the King of mankind, the God of mankind, from the evil of the whisperer who withdraws, who whispers in the hearts of mankind, from among jinn and mankind.

Urdu Translation

کہہ دیجیے: میں لوگوں کے رب، لوگوں کے بادشاہ، لوگوں کے معبود کی پناہ لیتا ہوں، وسوسہ ڈالنے والے، پیچھے ہٹ جانے والے کے شر سے، جو لوگوں کے دلوں میں وسوسے ڈالتا ہے، خواہ وہ جنوں میں سے ہو یا انسانوں میں سے۔

Reference: Qur'an 114:1–6 (Surah An-Nas).

3. Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas

The three short surahs — Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas — are among the most important daily recitations for a Muslim seeking protection.

A well-known Sunnah practice is to recite these three surahs three times in the morning and evening. They are also recited before sleeping as part of the prophetic practice of seeking Allah's protection.

Simple daily practice:
  • Surah Al-Ikhlas — 3 times
  • Surah Al-Falaq — 3 times
  • Surah An-Nas — 3 times

Try to recite them with attention and understanding rather than simply rushing through the words.

4. Dua: Bismillahil-Ladhi La Yadurru

This is a beautiful masnoon dua for seeking protection from harm. The Prophet ﷺ taught that whoever says it three times in the morning and three times in the evening will not be harmed by anything, by Allah's permission.

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

Transliteration

Bismillahil-ladhi la yadurru ma'asmihi shay'un fil-ardi wa la fis-sama'i, wa Huwas-Sami'ul-'Alim.

English Meaning

In the name of Allah, with whose name nothing in the earth or in the heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

Urdu Translation

اللہ کے نام کے ساتھ، جس کے نام کے ساتھ زمین اور آسمان میں کوئی چیز نقصان نہیں پہنچا سکتی، اور وہ خوب سننے والا، خوب جاننے والا ہے۔

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi and Sunan Abi Dawud; reported in Riyad as-Salihin 1457.

5. Ruqyah Dua for Evil Eye and Envy

Among the beautiful supplications used for ruqyah is a dua seeking Allah's healing and protection from anything harmful, including the evil eye and the envy of an envier.

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ، مِنْ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ يُؤْذِيكَ، مِنْ شَرِّ كُلِّ نَفْسٍ أَوْ عَيْنِ حَاسِدٍ، اللَّهُ يَشْفِيكَ، بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ

Transliteration

Bismillahi arqika, min kulli shay'in yu'dhika, min sharri kulli nafsin aw 'ayni hasidin, Allahu yashfika, bismillahi arqika.

English Meaning

In the name of Allah, I perform ruqyah for you from everything that harms you, from the evil of every soul or the envious eye. May Allah heal you. In the name of Allah, I perform ruqyah for you.

Urdu Translation

اللہ کے نام کے ساتھ میں تم پر دم کرتا ہوں، ہر اس چیز سے جو تمہیں تکلیف دے، ہر جان کے شر سے یا حسد کرنے والی نظر کے شر سے۔ اللہ تمہیں شفا دے۔ اللہ کے نام کے ساتھ میں تم پر دم کرتا ہوں۔

Reference: Sahih Muslim, also reported in Riyad as-Salihin 908.

6. Dua for Protection of Children

Parents naturally worry about their children. The Sunnah includes a beautiful supplication for seeking Allah's protection for children.

أُعِيذُكُمَا بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّةِ، مِنْ كُلِّ شَيْطَانٍ وَهَامَّةٍ، وَمِنْ كُلِّ عَيْنٍ لَامَّةٍ

Transliteration

U'idhukuma bi kalimatillahit-tammati, min kulli shaytanin wa hammatin, wa min kulli 'aynin lammah.

English Meaning

I seek protection for you both in the perfect words of Allah from every devil, every poisonous creature, and every harmful evil eye.

Urdu Translation

میں تم دونوں کو اللہ کے کامل کلمات کے ذریعے ہر شیطان، ہر نقصان پہنچانے والی مخلوق اور ہر نقصان دہ نظر سے اللہ کی پناہ میں دیتا ہوں۔

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 3371.

7. How to Protect Yourself from the Evil Eye According to Islam

Islam teaches us to protect ourselves through worship and remembrance rather than fear and superstition.

  • Keep your five daily prayers.
  • Recite Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas regularly.
  • Read the morning and evening adhkar.
  • Make sincere dua to Allah.
  • Keep your heart attached to Allah and trust Him.
  • When admiring someone's blessing, remember Allah and ask Him to bless it.
  • Do not constantly suspect family members, friends or neighbours of envy.
  • Avoid superstitions and practices that have no basis in Islam.

When Should You Read These Duas?

Morning

  • Recite Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas three times.
  • Read the masnoon morning adhkar.
  • Recite the protection dua beginning with “Bismillahil-ladhi la yadurru…” three times.

Evening

  • Repeat the three Quls.
  • Read the evening adhkar.
  • Make dua for yourself, your family and your children.

Before Sleeping

  • Recite Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas.
  • Seek Allah's protection with a sincere heart.
  • Remember that your safety is ultimately in Allah's hands.

How to Perform Simple Ruqyah for Yourself

Ruqyah is not something mysterious or frightening. A Muslim can recite Qur'anic verses and authentic supplications and ask Allah for protection and healing.

  1. Begin with the name of Allah.
  2. Recite Qur'anic verses and authentic duas.
  3. Ask Allah sincerely for protection and healing.
  4. Keep your heart focused on Allah rather than the person or thing you fear.
  5. Continue your normal religious and practical responsibilities.

The Prophet ﷺ approved ruqyah when it did not contain anything contrary to Islam. Therefore, ruqyah should be based on permissible words and sincere reliance upon Allah.

Should We Be Afraid of Everyone's Evil Eye?

No. A believer should not live in constant fear.

The existence of the evil eye does not mean that every illness, problem or difficulty is caused by someone's gaze or jealousy. Sometimes problems have ordinary physical, emotional, financial or social causes.

Islam teaches balance: make dua, use the means Allah has provided, seek appropriate help when needed, and place your trust in Allah.

Remember: Protection does not come from an object, charm or person. A Muslim asks Allah for protection and relies upon Him.

What About Taweez, Charms and Superstitions?

Muslims should be careful about practices that are not established in the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah. Protection should not be based on superstition, fear or objects believed to possess independent power.

The safest approach is to follow the clear guidance of the Qur'an and Sunnah: prayer, remembrance, authentic duas, Qur'an recitation and sincere reliance upon Allah.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best dua for protection from the evil eye?

Among the important protections are Surah Al-Falaq and Surah An-Nas, along with authentic ruqyah supplications taught in the Sunnah. A believer should regularly seek Allah's protection rather than relying on one isolated phrase only.

Can I read these duas for my children?

Yes. The Sunnah includes seeking Allah's protection for children. Parents can make authentic supplications for their children and ask Allah to keep them safe from every harm.

How many times should I read Surah Al-Falaq and An-Nas?

The three Quls — Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas — are commonly recited three times in the morning and evening as part of the Sunnah of protection.

Can I perform ruqyah on myself?

Yes. A Muslim may recite Qur'an and authentic supplications over themselves and ask Allah for protection and healing.

Is the evil eye real in Islam?

Yes. Authentic hadith affirm the reality of the evil eye. However, Muslims should not become obsessed with it or blame people without evidence.

What should I do if I think I have been affected by the evil eye?

Turn to Allah through prayer, dua, Qur'an recitation and authentic ruqyah. Continue taking appropriate practical steps for any physical or personal problem rather than assuming every difficulty is caused by the evil eye.

A Beautiful Daily Protection Routine

If you want a simple routine, do not make it complicated. Begin your day by remembering Allah and finish your day by returning to Him.

  1. Pray Fajr and maintain the five daily prayers.
  2. Read your morning adhkar.
  3. Recite Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas three times.
  4. Read the protection dua beginning with “Bismillahil-ladhi la yadurru…”
  5. In the evening, repeat the relevant adhkar and Qur'anic protections.
  6. Before sleeping, recite the three Quls and make sincere dua.
  7. Teach your children simple authentic duas from an early age.

Final Reminder: Put Your Trust in Allah

The greatest protection for a believer is not living in fear. It is living with tawakkul — trusting Allah.

The Qur'an teaches us beautiful words of refuge, and the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught his companions authentic supplications for protection and healing. These teachings give the believer something precious: a way to turn fear into dua and anxiety into trust.

When you feel worried about the evil eye, envy or any kind of harm, remember that Allah is greater than everything you fear. Ask Him sincerely, remember Him, follow the Sunnah and keep your heart connected to Him.

May Allah protect us and our families from every form of evil, envy, harm and hardship. May He fill our homes with Qur'an, peace, mercy and barakah.

Ameen.
