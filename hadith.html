📖 About Hadith

Hadith are reports describing the sayings, actions, approvals and teachings of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. They are an important source for understanding the Sunnah alongside the Quran.

ہر حدیث کو سمجھتے وقت اس کے متن، سند، درجہ اور محدثین کی تشریحات کو بھی دیکھنا ضروری ہے۔

Understanding Hadith

A few important principles when reading Hadith.

1. Check the Source

Always note which Hadith collection contains the report and check its reference.

2. Understand the Context

A Hadith should be understood together with its surrounding narrations, chapter and scholarly explanation.

3. Be Careful With Fiqh

Different scholars may understand evidence differently. Differences should be presented respectfully with proper references.
