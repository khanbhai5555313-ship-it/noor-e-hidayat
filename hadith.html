📚 The Six Major Hadith Collections

The Kutub al-Sittah refers to six major Hadith collections widely studied in Sunni Islamic scholarship: Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Sunan an-Nasa'i and Sunan Ibn Majah.
