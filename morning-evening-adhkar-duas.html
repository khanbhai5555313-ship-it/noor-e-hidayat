Morning and Evening Adhkar: Authentic Daily Duas

Daily Remembrance of Allah

What Are Morning and Evening Adhkar?

Morning and evening adhkar are supplications and remembrance of Allah that Muslims can recite as part of their daily worship. They help a believer remember Allah throughout the day and seek His guidance, mercy and protection.

The following examples include well-known supplications from authentic Islamic sources.

Ayat al-Kursi

Ayat al-Kursi is found in Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 255. It is among the important verses that Muslims recite in their daily remembrance.

Quran Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:255

You can read the full Arabic text and English translation on our Ayatul Kursi article .

Morning and Evening Dua for Protection

One authentic supplication reported in the Sunnah asks Allah for well-being and protection in this world and the Hereafter.

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ

English Meaning

O Allah, I ask You for pardon and well-being in this world and the Hereafter.

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud 5074

Sayyid al-Istighfar

Sayyid al-Istighfar is a well-known supplication for seeking forgiveness from Allah. It is reported in Sahih al-Bukhari.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught this supplication and described its great virtue when recited with sincere faith.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6306

Tip: Learn the dua carefully, understand its meaning and recite it sincerely rather than treating remembrance as merely a routine.

Three Quls for Daily Protection

Surah Al-Ikhlas, Surah Al-Falaq and Surah An-Nas are commonly recited as part of morning and evening remembrance.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught their recitation three times in the morning and evening.

Reference: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3575

Why Practice Morning and Evening Adhkar?

Daily remembrance helps Muslims keep Allah in their thoughts and strengthens their connection with Him. The purpose of adhkar is worship, remembrance and sincere supplication.

Muslims should also learn the meanings of the duas and try to understand what they are asking from Allah.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are morning adhkar?

Morning adhkar are Quranic verses, supplications and forms of remembrance recited during the morning.

What are evening adhkar?

Evening adhkar are supplications and remembrance recited during the evening as part of a Muslim's daily worship.

Can I read adhkar every day?

Yes. Regular remembrance of Allah is encouraged in Islam, and authentic supplications can be included in one's daily routine.
