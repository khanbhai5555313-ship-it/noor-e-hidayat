Namaz ka tarika kya hai? Namaz kaise padhein? Namaz Islam ki sabse aham ibadaton mein se ek hai. Allah Ta'ala ne momineen par namaz ko muqarrar auqat mein farz kiya hai.

Hadith:

Rasulullah ﷺ ne farmaya:

“Namaz us tarah padho jaise tumne mujhe namaz padhte dekha hai.”

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 631

Namaz Se Pehle Kya Zaroori Hai?

Namaz se pehle in basic cheezon ka khayal rakhein:

  1. Wudu ho.
  2. Badan aur kapde paak hon.
  3. Namaz ki jagah paak ho.
  4. Namaz ka waqt shuru ho chuka ho.
  5. Qibla ki taraf rukh ho.
  6. Satr dhaka hua ho.
  7. Dil mein namaz ki niyyat ho.

Namaz Kaise Shuru Karein?

1. Qibla Ki Taraf Khade Hon

Qibla ki taraf khade ho jayein aur dil mein jis namaz ko Allah ke liye ada karna hai uska irada karein.

Niyyat: Niyyat asal mein dil ke irade ka naam hai. Kisi fixed Arabic jumle ko lazmi samajhna zaroori nahi.

2. Takbeer-e-Tahrima

Namaz shuru karte waqt Allahu Akbar kahen.

اللّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ

Allahu Akbar

Meaning: Allah sabse bada hai.

Qiyam Aur Quran Ki Qiraat

Takbeer ke baad qiyam mein khade hokar Quran ki qiraat ki jati hai. Namaz ke shuru mein Sana padhna mashhoor masnoon amal hai.

سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَى جَدُّكَ، وَلَا إِلَهَ غَيْرُكَ

Subhanak Allahumma wa bihamdika, wa tabarakasmuka, wa ta'ala jadduka, wa la ilaha ghairuk.

Ae Allah! Tu paak hai aur main teri hamd karta hoon. Tera naam barkat wala hai, teri shaan buland hai aur tere siwa koi mabood nahi.

Ta'awwuz

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ

A'udhu billahi minash-shaitanir-rajim.

Bismillah

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim.

Surah Al-Fatihah

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ
الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ
إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ
صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ
غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ

Alhamdu lillahi Rabbil-'alamin. Ar-Rahmanir-Rahim. Maliki Yawmid-Din. Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in. Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim. Siratal-ladhina an'amta 'alayhim. Ghairil-maghdubi 'alayhim wa laddallin.

Meaning: Sab tareef Allah ke liye hai jo tamam jahano ka Rabb hai. Hum sirf usi ki ibadat karte hain aur usi se madad mangte hain. Humein seedha rasta dikha.

Surah Al-Fatihah Ke Baad

Surah Al-Fatihah ke baad Quran ki koi surah ya ayat padhi ja sakti hai. Beginners ke liye Surah Al-Ikhlas yaad karna aasaan ho sakta hai.

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ
لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ

Qul huwallahu ahad. Allahus-samad. Lam yalid wa lam yulad. Wa lam yakul-lahu kufuwan ahad.

Ruku Ka Tarika

Quran ki qiraat ke baad Allahu Akbar kahte hue ruku mein jayein. Ruku mein sukoon aur itminan rakhein.

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ

Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azim.

Meaning: Mera Rabb, jo Azeem hai, paak hai.

Ruku Se Uthna

سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ

Sami'Allahu liman hamidah.

Seedhe khade hone ke baad:

رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ

Rabbana wa lakal-hamd.

Sajde Ka Tarika

Allahu Akbar kahte hue sajde mein jayein. Sajde mein Allah ke saamne khushu aur aajizi ke saath rahen.

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى

Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la.

Meaning: Mera Rabb, jo sabse buland hai, paak hai.

Do Sajdon Ke Darmiyan

Pehle sajde ke baad Allahu Akbar kahte hue baithen. Sukoon se baith kar doosre sajde mein jayein.

Doosra Sajda

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى

Doosre sajde ke baad pehli rakat complete hoti hai.

Doosri Rakat

Doosre sajde ke baad doosri rakat ke liye khade ho jayein. Doosri rakat mein Surah Al-Fatihah aur Quran ki qiraat ke baad ruku aur dono sajde ada karein.

Attahiyyat

Doosri rakat ke doosre sajde ke baad baith kar Attahiyyat padhi jati hai:

التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَى عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ.

At-tahiyyatu lillahi was-salawatu wat-tayyibat. As-salamu 'alayka ayyuhan-nabiyyu wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. As-salamu 'alayna wa 'ala 'ibadillahis-salihin. Ashhadu alla ilaha illallahu wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh.

Durood Ibrahim

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

Durood ke baad masnoon dua padhi ja sakti hai.

Salam Pherna

Aakhir mein right aur left taraf salam pher kar namaz complete ki jati hai.

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ

Assalamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah.

5 Waqt Ki Namaz Ki Farz Rakat

Namaz Farz Rakat
Fajr 2
Zuhr 4
Asr 4
Maghrib 3
Isha 4
Total 17

3 Rakat Namaz Kaise Padhein?

Maghrib ki 3 farz rakat hoti hain. Pehli aur doosri rakat ke baad doosri rakat par qa'dah hota hai. Phir teesri rakat ke liye khade hote hain. Aakhri qa'dah mein Attahiyyat, Durood aur dua ke baad salam pherte hain.

4 Rakat Namaz Kaise Padhein?

Zuhr aur Asr ki farz namaz 4 rakat hoti hai. Pehli aur doosri rakat ke baad qa'dah hota hai. Phir teesri aur chauthi rakat ada ki jati hain. Aakhri qa'dah mein Attahiyyat, Durood aur dua ke baad salam pherte hain.

Namaz Mein Khushu Kaise Paida Karein?

  • Allah ki azmat ko yaad karein.
  • Quran aur duao ka meaning samajhne ki koshish karein.
  • Ruku aur sajde mein jaldi na karein.
  • Namaz ke waqt phone aur distractions se bachhein.
  • Namaz ko sirf routine nahi, Allah ki ibadat samjhein.

Namaz Mein Aam Galtiyan

  • Ruku aur sajde mein bahut jaldi karna.
  • Namaz ke words ko galat pronunciation ke saath padhna.
  • Quran ki qiraat ko seekhne ki koshish na karna.
  • Namaz ko jaan-boojh kar delay karna.
  • Fiqhi differences par unnecessary behas karna.
Important: Nabi ﷺ ne ek shakhs ko namaz dobara padhne ka hukm diya aur ruku, qiyam aur sajde mein itminan se ada karne ki taleem di.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 397a.

Agar Namaz Yaad Nahi Hai To Kya Karein?

  1. Sabse pehle wudu seekhein.
  2. Surah Al-Fatihah yaad karein.
  3. Ek chhoti surah yaad karein.
  4. Ruku ki tasbeeh seekhein.
  5. Sajde ki tasbeeh seekhein.
  6. Attahiyyat seekhein.
  7. Durood Ibrahim seekhein.
  8. 2 rakat namaz ka practice karein.
  9. Phir 3 aur 4 rakat namazon ko samjhein.

Namaz Ke Baad

Namaz ke baad Allah ka zikr karein aur apni zarooriyat ke liye Allah Ta'ala se dua karein. Hidayat, maghfirat, imaan, rizq aur duniya-o-akhirat ki bhalai maang sakte hain.

Namaz Aur Quran

Namaz mein Quran ki qiraat hoti hai. Isliye Quran ko padhna aur uska meaning samajhna namaz mein khushu aur samajh ko behtar bana sakta hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Namaz kaise padhein?

Namaz niyyat aur takbeer se shuru hoti hai. Qiyam, Quran ki qiraat, ruku, sajda aur qa'dah ke marahil ada kiye jate hain aur aakhir mein salam pher kar namaz complete ki jati hai.

5 waqt ki namaz mein kitni farz rakat hain?

Fajr 2, Zuhr 4, Asr 4, Maghrib 3 aur Isha 4 farz rakat hain. Kul 17 farz rakat hoti hain.

Namaz ke liye niyyat kaise karein?

Niyyat dil ke irade ka naam hai. Dil mein Allah ke liye jis namaz ko ada karna hai uska irada karein.

Ruku mein kya padhein?

Ruku mein “Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azim” padhna mashhoor masnoon tasbeeh hai.

Sajde mein kya padhein?

Sajde mein “Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la” padhna mashhoor masnoon tasbeeh hai.

Fajr ki farz namaz kitni rakat hai?

Fajr ki 2 farz rakat hain.

Zuhr ki farz namaz kitni rakat hai?

Zuhr ki 4 farz rakat hain.

Asr ki farz namaz kitni rakat hai?

Asr ki 4 farz rakat hain.

Maghrib ki farz namaz kitni rakat hai?

Maghrib ki 3 farz rakat hain.

Isha ki farz namaz kitni rakat hai?

Isha ki 4 farz rakat hain.

Important Islamic Note

Namaz ke kuch detailed masail mein mukhtalif fiqhi madhahib ke darmiyan differences milte hain. Isliye kisi specific madhhab ke detailed rulings ke liye qualified scholar se mashwara karna behtar hai.
