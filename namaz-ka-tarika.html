Namaz Guide

Namaz Ka Tarika – Step by Step

Namaz (Salah) Islam ki sabse important ibadaton mein se ek hai. Har baligh Muslim par paanch waqt ki namaz farz hai.

This guide explains the basic method of performing Salah step by step, including Takbeer, Qiyam, Ruku, Sajdah, Attahiyyat, Durood, Dua and Salam.

Important:
Namaz ko sukoon, khushu aur Allah ki taraf poori tawajjoh ke saath ada karna chahiye.

This is a general learning guide. For detailed differences in fiqh, follow a reliable qualified scholar of your madhhab.
Five Daily Prayers

5 Waqt Ki Namaz Ki Rakats

Prayer Rakat Basic Order
Fajr 2 Sunnah + 2 Farz 2 + 2
Dhuhr 4 Sunnah + 4 Farz + 2 Sunnah 4 + 4 + 2
Asr 4 Sunnah + 4 Farz 4 + 4
Maghrib 3 Farz + 2 Sunnah 3 + 2
Isha 4 Sunnah + 4 Farz + 2 Sunnah + Witr 4 + 4 + 2 + Witr
Note: Rakat details can vary depending on the school of jurisprudence and whether one is referring to Sunnah, Farz, Witr or Nafl. Follow your trusted scholar for the details of your madhhab.
Step 1

Niyyat – Niyyah

Sabse pehle dil mein jis namaz ko padhna hai uski niyyat karein. Niyyat ka asal maqam dil hai.

First make the intention in your heart for the particular prayer you are about to perform.

Niyyat ko dil mein karna zaroori hai. Kisi fixed Arabic sentence ko zabaan se kehna har situation mein lazim nahi hai.
Step 2

Takbeer-e-Tahrima

Qiblah ki taraf rukh karke namaz shuru karein aur haath utha kar Allahu Akbar kahen.

اللهُ أَكْبَرُ
Transliteration: Allahu Akbar
Meaning: Allah is the Greatest.
Hindi: Allah sabse bada hai.
Step 3

Qiyam – Standing

Takbeer ke baad qiyam mein khade hokar haath bandhein aur Sana padhein.

Sana

سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَى جَدُّكَ، وَلَا إِلٰهَ غَيْرُكَ
Subhanak Allahumma wa bihamdika, wa tabarakasmuka, wa ta'ala jadduka, wa la ilaha ghayruk.
English: Glory is to You, O Allah, and all praise is Yours. Blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, and there is no god but You.

Hindi: Aye Allah! Tu paak hai aur sab tareef tere liye hai. Tera naam barkat wala hai, teri shaan buland hai, aur tere siwa koi mabood nahi.
Step 4

Ta'awwuz and Bismillah

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ
A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim.
English: I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan.

Hindi: Main Allah ki panah maangta hoon shaitaan-e-mardood se.
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim.
English: In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Especially Merciful.

Hindi: Allah ke naam se jo nihayat meherban, baar baar rahm karne wala hai.
Step 5

Surah Al-Fatihah

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ
الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ
إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ
صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ
غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ
Alhamdu lillahi rabbil-'alamin.
Ar-Rahmanir-Rahim.
Maliki yawmid-din.
Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in.
Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim.
Siratal-ladhina an'amta 'alayhim.
Ghayril-maghdubi 'alayhim wa lad-dallin.
English: All praise is for Allah, Lord of the worlds. The Most Merciful, the Especially Merciful. Master of the Day of Judgment. You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed Your favor, not of those who earned Your anger, nor of those who went astray.

Hindi: Sab tareef Allah ke liye hai jo tamam jahano ka Rab hai. Woh nihayat meherban aur baar baar rahm karne wala hai. Qayamat ke din ka Malik hai. Hum sirf Teri hi ibadat karte hain aur sirf Tujh hi se madad maangte hain. Hamein seedha raasta dikha. Un logon ka raasta jin par Tune inaam kiya, na unka jin par ghazab hua aur na gumrahon ka.

Ameen

Step 6

Surah After Al-Fatihah

Surah Al-Fatihah ke baad Quran ki koi surah ya kuch ayaat padhi ja sakti hain, according to the prayer and your school of fiqh.

Example: Surah Al-Ikhlas

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ
لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ
Qul huwallahu ahad.
Allahus-samad.
Lam yalid wa lam yulad.
Wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan ahad.
English: Say: He is Allah, One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, nor is there to Him any equivalent.

Hindi: Keh dijiye: Woh Allah ek hai. Allah be-niyaz hai. Na usne kisi ko janam diya aur na woh paida hua. Aur koi uske barabar nahi.
Step 7

Ruku

Quran ki tilawat ke baad Allahu Akbar keh kar ruku mein jayein.

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azim.
English: Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great.

Hindi: Paak hai mera Rab, jo sabse azeem hai.

Is tasbeeh ko aam tor par teen martaba ya us se zyada padha jata hai.

Step 8

Qawmah – Standing After Ruku

Ruku se uthte waqt yeh kaha jata hai:

سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ
Sami'allahu liman hamidah.
English: Allah hears the one who praises Him.

Hindi: Allah uski sunta hai jo uski tareef karta hai.

Seedha khade hone ke baad kaha jata hai:

رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ
Rabbana wa lakal-hamd.
English: Our Lord, and to You belongs all praise.

Hindi: Aye hamare Rab! Aur tere hi liye sab tareef hai.
Step 9

Sajdah

Allahu Akbar keh kar sajdah mein jayein.

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la.
English: Glory be to my Lord, the Most High.

Hindi: Paak hai mera Rab, jo sabse buland hai.

Sajdah mein is tasbeeh ko aam tor par teen martaba ya us se zyada padha jata hai.

Step 10

Jalsa – Sitting Between Two Sajdahs

Pehle sajdah ke baad Allahu Akbar keh kar baithhein, phir doosra sajdah karein.

Dua

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي
Rabbighfir li.
English: My Lord, forgive me.

Hindi: Aye mere Rab! Mujhe bakhsh de.
Step 11

Second Rakat

Doosre sajdah ke baad doosri rakat ke liye khade ho jayein.

Doosri rakat mein qiyam, Surah Al-Fatihah aur Quran ki tilawat, phir ruku aur dono sajde kiye jate hain.

Doosri rakat ke baad qa'dah mein baith kar Attahiyyat padhi jati hai.

Qa'dah

Attahiyyat

التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَىٰ عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ
Attahiyyatu lillahi was-salawatu wat-tayyibat.
Assalamu 'alayka ayyuhan-nabiyyu wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.
Assalamu 'alayna wa 'ala 'ibadillahis-salihin.
Ashhadu alla ilaha illallah,
wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh.
English: All greetings, prayers and pure words are for Allah. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of Allah. I bear witness that there is no deity except Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger.

Hindi: Tamam ta'zeem, ibadatein aur paak baatein Allah ke liye hain. Aye Nabi! Aap par salam, Allah ki rahmat aur uski barkatein hon. Hum par aur Allah ke nek bandon par salam ho. Main gawahi deta hoon ke Allah ke siwa koi mabood nahi, aur main gawahi deta hoon ke Muhammad ﷺ Allah ke bande aur Rasool hain.
Durood

Durood Ibrahim

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ. اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ
Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Muhammad, kama sallayta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala ali Ibrahim, innaka Hamidum Majid.

Allahumma barik 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Muhammad, kama barakta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala ali Ibrahim, innaka Hamidum Majid.
English: O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.

O Allah, bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.

Hindi: Aye Allah! Muhammad ﷺ aur Aal-e-Muhammad par rahmat bhej, jis tarah Tune Ibrahim aur Aal-e-Ibrahim par rahmat bheji. Beshak Tu tareef ke laayak aur buzurg hai.

Aye Allah! Muhammad ﷺ aur Aal-e-Muhammad mein barkat ata farma, jis tarah Tune Ibrahim aur Aal-e-Ibrahim mein barkat ata farmayi. Beshak Tu tareef ke laayak aur buzurg hai.
Dua

Dua After Durood

Durood ke baad masnoon dua padhna sunnat se sabit hai. Ek mashhoor dua yeh hai:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ جَهَنَّمَ، وَمِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ، وَمِنْ فِتْنَةِ الْمَحْيَا وَالْمَمَاتِ، وَمِنْ شَرِّ فِتْنَةِ الْمَسِيحِ الدَّجَّالِ
Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min 'adhabi jahannam, wa min 'adhabil-qabr, wa min fitnatil-mahya wal-mamat, wa min sharri fitnatil-masihid-dajjal.
English: O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the punishment of Hell, from the punishment of the grave, from the trials of life and death, and from the evil trial of the False Messiah.

Hindi: Aye Allah! Main Jahannam ke azaab se, qabr ke azaab se, zindagi aur maut ke fitnon se, aur Masih Dajjal ke fitne ke shar se Teri panah maangta hoon.
Final Step

Salam – Ending the Prayer

Dua ke baad pehle right side aur phir left side salam phera jata hai.

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ
Assalamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah.
English: Peace and the mercy of Allah be upon you.

Hindi: Aap par Allah ki salamati aur rahmat ho.

Is tarah namaz mukammal hoti hai.

Quick Summary

2 Rakat Namaz Ka Short Step-by-Step Tarika

  1. Wudu karke paak jagah par Qiblah ki taraf khade hon.
  2. Dil mein namaz ki niyyat karein.
  3. Takbeer-e-Tahrima keh kar namaz shuru karein: Allahu Akbar.
  4. Qiyam mein Sana padhein.
  5. Ta'awwuz, Bismillah aur Surah Al-Fatihah padhein.
  6. Surah Al-Fatihah ke baad Quran ki kuch ayaat/surah padhein jahan applicable ho.
  7. Ruku mein jayein aur Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azim padhein.
  8. Ruku se uth kar qawmah mein khade hon.
  9. Pehla sajdah karein aur Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la padhein.
  10. Dono sajdon ke darmiyan baithhein.
  11. Doosra sajdah karein.
  12. Doosri rakat ke liye khade hon.
  13. Doosri rakat complete karke Attahiyyat ke liye baithhein.
  14. Attahiyyat, Durood Ibrahim aur masnoon dua padhein.
  15. Right aur left side salam pher kar namaz mukammal karein.
Important

Namaz Ke Liye Zaroori Baatein

  • Wudu ya required purification ka ehtimam karein.
  • Kapde aur namaz ki jagah paak hon.
  • Qiblah ki taraf rukh karein.
  • Namaz apne waqt par ada karein.
  • Namaz mein khushu aur tawajjoh paida karne ki koshish karein.
  • Ruku aur sajdah ko itminan aur sukoon ke saath ada karein.
  • Namaz ke faraiz aur wajibat ko apne trusted scholar se bhi seekhein.
Conclusion

Namaz Allah Se Connection Hai

Namaz sirf ek routine nahi balki Allah ke saath bande ka direct connection hai.

Prayer ko samajh kar aur khushu ke saath ada karne se insaan Allah ki yaad mein rehta hai aur apni zindagi ko behtar banane ki koshish karta hai.

May Allah accept our prayers, forgive our mistakes and guide us to the straight path. Ameen.
