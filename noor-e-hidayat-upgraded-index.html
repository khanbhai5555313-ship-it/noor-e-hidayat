🤲 Daily Duas

Dua for Knowledge

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

Transliteration: Rabbi zidni ilma.

Meaning: My Lord, increase me in knowledge.

Quran 20:114

Dua for Parents

رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا

Transliteration: Rabbir hamhuma kama rabbayani sagheera.

Meaning: My Lord, have mercy on them as they raised me when I was small.

Quran 17:24

Dua for Good

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Meaning: Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

Quran 2:201

🕌 Seerah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

Early Life

Muhammad ﷺ was born in Makkah among Quraysh. Islamic sources describe his early life, family, honesty and trustworthy character.

Beginning of Revelation

At the age of forty, the first revelation came to him in the Cave of Hira through Angel Jibril عليه السلام. The opening revelation is associated with Surah Al-‘Alaq.

Migration to Madinah

The Hijrah from Makkah to Madinah became a major turning point in the history of the Muslim community and later marked the beginning of the Islamic calendar.

His Character

The Quran describes the Prophet ﷺ as being upon a عظیم character (Quran 68:4). His life teaches mercy, patience, honesty, justice and worship of Allah.

💡 Islamic Knowledge

Why Is Salah Important?

Salah is one of the central acts of worship in Islam. Muslims establish prayer to remember Allah, seek His help and maintain a regular connection with Him.

Good Character

Islam places strong emphasis on honesty, kindness, patience, keeping promises, helping others and avoiding harm.

Seeking Beneficial Knowledge

Learning the Quran and Sunnah, asking qualified scholars when unsure, and acting upon beneficial knowledge are important parts of a Muslim’s growth.

Religious rulings can differ by school and scholarly interpretation. For personal fatwa or complicated matters, consult a qualified and trusted scholar.
