Quran & Islamic Reminder

Quran Mein Sabr Ki Ahmiyat

Sabr Islam ki ek azeem sifat hai. Zindagi mein mushkilaat, pareshaniyan aur imtihan aate rehte hain. Quran Majeed humein sikhata hai ke mushkil waqt mein Allah par bharosa rakhte hue sabr aur istiqamat ka raasta ikhtiyar karna chahiye.

Quran Mein Sabr Ka Zikr

Quran Majeed mein Allah Ta'ala ne kai jagah sabr karne walon ki fazilat bayan ki hai. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai ke Allah sabr karne walon ke saath hai.

Quran Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:153

Mushkil Waqt Mein Sabr

Jab insaan kisi mushkil ya pareshani ka saamna karta hai to ghabrahat ke bajaye Allah ki taraf ruju karna chahiye. Sabr ka matlab yeh nahi ke insaan apni pareshani ko mehsoos na kare, balki yeh hai ke woh Allah ki ita'at par qayam rahe aur na-umeedi se bache.

Sabr Aur Namaz

Quran mein Allah Ta'ala ne mushkilaat ke waqt sabr aur namaz se madad lene ki hidayat di hai. Isse humein pata chalta hai ke mushkil waqt mein Allah ki ibadat aur uski taraf ruju karna momin ke liye bohat aham hai.

Quran Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:153

Sabr Ka Matlab Kya Hai?

  • Allah ki ita'at par qayam rehna
  • Mushkilaat mein na-umeed na hona
  • Gunahon se bachne ki koshish karna
  • Allah ke faislon par bharosa rakhna
  • Mushkil waqt mein dua aur namaz ki taraf ruju karna

Allah Sabr Karne Walon Ke Saath Hai

Sabr momin ko mushkil waqt mein mazboot banata hai. Jab insaan Allah par bharosa karta hai aur uski ita'at par qayam rehta hai, to uske dil mein umeed aur sukoon paida hota hai.

Quran Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:153

Conclusion

Quran humein sabr, tawakkul aur Allah ki taraf ruju karne ki taleem deta hai. Zindagi ke mushkil halaat mein humein Allah se madad maangni chahiye, namaz aur dua ko mazboot pakadna chahiye aur Allah ki rehmat se mayoos nahi hona chahiye.
